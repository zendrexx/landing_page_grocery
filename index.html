Features

Built around what you have,
what you want, and your budget

Six things the app does — all real, all shipping. No barcode scanning, no calorie guessing, no gimmicks.

Plans from your pantry

Your pantry already knows what's for dinner. Plans reuse what you own, prefer items nearing expiry, and skip duplicate buys — so less ends up in the bin.

  • Categories, quantities, search & a quick +/- stepper
  • Expiry tracking: “expires in 3 days”, “running low”
  • Opt-in reminders the day before something expires

Fits your goals

Macros that aren't a guess. Calorie and protein / fat / carb targets are computed deterministically (Mifflin-St Jeor → TDEE → goal-adjusted) and tracked daily.

  • Goals for gain muscle, lose fat, or maintain
  • Diet type, allergies, favorites & avoids respected
  • The AI reasons over the numbers — it never invents them

Always on budget

The grocery list auto-fills from your plan with only what the pantry doesn't cover — kept inside your weekly ₱ budget, with a plain-language note on how it got there.

  • Money-saving substitution suggestions
  • Grounded in crowdsourced local prices — real ₱ per unit near you
  • A clear “Within budget ✓” — or exactly what to swap

Ask Zeb, anytime

Zeb isn't a sticker — he's the assistant inside the app. Ask him what to cook tonight, how to stretch the budget, or what to do with the veg that's about to turn. He can see your pantry, your plan and your budget, so the answer is about your kitchen.

  • “What can I cook right now?” — answered from what you own
  • Tap-to-ask chips for cheapest dinners and quick ideas
  • He speaks up first too — expiry warnings on Home, low-stock notes on your list

Snap to stock

Photograph a receipt or your groceries and the AI reads the items and adds them to your pantry — and can log the spend at the same time.

  • Receipt → items added & spend logged
  • Shelf or haul photo → items spotted and stocked
  • No barcode scanning, no manual typing marathon

Why it's smart

Calorie and macro targets come from Mifflin-St Jeor BMR → TDEE → goal adjustment. The AI reasons over that model — it doesn't guess calorie numbers.

Pantry-first, less waste

Every plan starts from what you already own and leans on items nearing expiry first — so food gets used, not thrown out.

Budgets on real prices

Zeb answers from your data

Ask Zeb reads your actual pantry, plan and budget before it replies — so it's advice about your kitchen this week, not generic internet cooking tips.

FAQ

Good questions

Does the AI just guess my calorie targets?

Targets are computed deterministically with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (BMR → TDEE → adjusted for your goal), not guessed. The AI reasons over those numbers to build recipes — it never invents calorie figures.

Is Zeb just a mascot?

No — Zeb is the assistant inside the app. There's an Ask Zeb chat you can open any time to ask what to cook right now, how to stretch the budget, or what to do with something about to expire, and he reads your real pantry, plan and budget before answering. He also speaks up on his own: expiry warnings on Home, low-stock notes on your grocery list, and a summary of what the AI will use before it generates a plan.

Do I have to type in my whole pantry by hand?

You can, but you don't have to. Snap a photo of a receipt or your shelf and the AI reads the items and adds them to your pantry — and can log the spend too.

Will the grocery list actually stay within my budget?

The list is built from your plan, contains only what your pantry doesn't already cover, and is kept within your weekly ₱ budget — with money-saving swaps when something's tight.

Where do the local prices come from?

From crowdsourced, anonymized community prices — real ₱ per unit near you — so budgets ride on real numbers instead of guesses.

Does it work offline and across devices?

It's offline-first, so it works without a connection, and your data syncs across devices when you're signed in.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes — every screen is built for light and dark, and you can pin either one or follow your system in Profile → Appearance. The screenshots on this page are real captures of both; use the Light / Dark switch above to flip them.

Is it only for the Philippines?

It's built PH-first, so prices and budgets are in peso (₱), but the planning, pantry and nutrition features work anywhere.
